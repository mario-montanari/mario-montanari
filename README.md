# Mario Montanari

**I design AI for the people who have to run a business with it.**

I was working on the web when the things we now call AI were quietly being
born inside it: Google's PageRank ordering the web by its links (1998), Amazon
learning what you'd want from the choices of people like you, Bayesian filters
classifying text instead of following hand-written rules. I didn't read about
that shift in a book. I was there, doing SEO and UX, watching it happen.

Today I work as an operational AI consultant and incubator, and operational is
the whole point: I design, build, and ship AI systems, agents, and automations
that run in production for professional firms and small businesses. Slides are
not a deliverable.

## What I'm building

[**Querymantic**](https://github.com/mario-montanari/querymantic): offline
demand intelligence for Claude Code and Claude Cowork. It maps the fan of
sub-queries behind AI search, the demand keyword tools can't see. Deterministic,
offline, every number traceable.

[**italiano-scrittura-anti-ai**](https://github.com/mario-montanari/italiano-scrittura-anti-ai):
a Claude skill for writing correct, human Italian. In Italian, for Italian
writers, on purpose.
